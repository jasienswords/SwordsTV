Queuey v5.0 · Ratings sync to your household when you’re signed in, and are kept on this device too. Review scores come from IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic, and are marked "Not yet verified" where a show couldn't be matched; unverified information is intentionally not invented. Posters, images and series details come from TMDB; U.S. streaming availability from JustWatch via TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Meet Queuey

An application buddy to simplify and personalize the complicated search for content that suits you best.

  • For You picks shows that fit your taste, and says why.
  • Rate It on any show teaches Queuey what you like.
  • My Queue keeps what you’re watching and what’s next in one place.
  • Taste Profile shows what Queuey has learned about you.
